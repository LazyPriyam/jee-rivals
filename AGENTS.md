# Agent Interaction Guidelines

## Scope & Turn Focus
- **Strict Turn Focus**: Address only the user's latest explicit message and its direct instructions.
- **No Historical Re-answering**: Do not re-investigate, re-fix, or re-explain previous issues, earlier resolved errors, or image attachments from prior steps unless the user explicitly asks about them in their current message.
- **Concise Communication**: Keep replies direct, concise, and focused strictly on the active task at hand.
